# Sector research layer
New user direction: work backwards from the customer job, value chain and difficult-to-replace constraint, then compare businesses and stock expectations separately.

Run python sector_analysis.py.
Read outputs/sector/PHOTONICS_VALUE_CHAIN.md and machine-readable comparison.json.

The first tested case covers COHR, LITE, FN, AAOI and IPGP. Sources are current primary disclosures read on 2026-11-16; sources without verified publication timestamps are capture-only and cannot be treated as historical knowledge.

## Architecture
sector job -> measurable technical constraints -> value-chain roles -> scoped competitor/substitute/cooperation hypotheses -> physical difficulty -> qualified supply scarcity -> switching friction -> economic capture -> durability -> market expectations/valuation.

The five gates are deliberately unknown until supported by appropriate evidence. Product marketing establishes a capability, not exclusive production capacity or price-setting power. Unknown is not zero; no numerical rank is calculated. Even fully supported gates yield a candidate, never an automatic monopoly.

Comparisons only identify overlapping scoped roles, not confirmed interchangeable products or customer-supplier relationships. COHR/IPGP industrial overlap differs from COHR/LITE/AAOI data-centre component overlap. Fabrinet is a manufacturing-layer business and requires a different comparator group. Alternative architectures, non-laser methods, customer integration and competing suppliers are explicit.

Each causal pathway includes first-, second- and third-order mechanisms with necessary conditions and falsifiers. No probabilities or stock returns have been assigned. Profit capture can reverse despite industry growth. Conditional CPO migration is not a blanket claim that pluggable optics suppliers lose.

## Human decision value
This changes the question from 'which photonics stock is best?' to 'which qualified capability is scarce, how could customers replace it, and who retains the economics?' Use the resulting evidence gaps to choose the next source to inspect. Check manufacturing yields, qualification times, contract structure, capacity utilisation, buyer concentration, capex and free cash flow before claiming rents.

## Scope
The sector dataset is a small manually sourced, reviewable example, not an exhaustive industry knowledge graph or independently verified technology benchmark. No capacities, gross margins, switching durations, market shares or valuation inputs are invented. Not a trade recommendation. The existing filings/expectations ledger remains separate; future orchestration should join by security identity without rewriting facts or converting scenarios into reported results.
